Voucher review

Each document has one button, Approve & import into TallyPrime. It approves all of the document's ready vouchers and creates them in TallyPrime straight away. Each voucher is first looked up by its marker, so nothing is posted twice. It is read back afterwards to verify it. Blocked vouchers are left out. To correct a voucher after the import, alter or delete it in TallyPrime.
