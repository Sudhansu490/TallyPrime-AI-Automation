Nothing is rejected automatically. Each possible duplicate shows the evidence field by field; a person decides. “Duplicate” means the later upload is the copy: a duplicate invoice is kept out of voucher generation, a duplicate bank transaction is left out of its statement's vouchers. Records that only share a date and an amount are not flagged. Every decision is recorded and can be reopened.
