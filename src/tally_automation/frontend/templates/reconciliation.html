Summary

Closing balance = opening balance + total credits − total debits, compared with the closing balance the statement prints. Each opening balance is also compared with the previous period's closing balance. Every figure comes from the reviewed statements and says where it came from; a figure the statements do not show is left blank, and a period without a statement is missing, never a zero balance. Differences are exact: ₹0.01 is a difference.
