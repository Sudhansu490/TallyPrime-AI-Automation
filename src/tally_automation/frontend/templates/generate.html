Voucher generation

Nothing is sent to TallyPrime. Generating builds the TallyPrime import XML on Computer A with the ledgers you choose and checks it against the retrieved masters. Importing is a separate step with explicit approval (Phase 8). Ledgers are pre-filled from matching rules (same GSTIN or account number, party names and keywords in the narration such as charges, TDS or cash) and from your earlier choices. Check every pre-filled ledger and change any that is wrong before generating.
